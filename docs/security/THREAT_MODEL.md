Master specification section 15 requires logs, runbooks and tickets to be treated as potentially untrusted input, and retrieved text to never override system policy or tool authorization. Section 6 forbids executing arbitrary model-generated production commands. These are the two highest-severity threat classes for this product and must be modelled first.
