# Production gap register

The single list of everything this repository does **not** do, or has not verified, that a real
production deployment would need. Other documents link here instead of keeping their own lists.
Each gap has one class, the evidence for its current state, and what would close it. Nothing
leaves this register because a document stopped mentioning it: a closed gap moves to
[Closed in the Phase 16 closure correction](#closed-in-the-phase-16-closure-correction) with the
evidence that closed it.

**Classes**

- **EXTERNAL PREREQUISITE** — needs infrastructure, accounts or people outside this repository.
- **NOT BUILT** — a capability that does not exist. The row states its impact and why it is absent.
- **NOT VERIFIED** — built, but only local evidence exists; it may work, it is not proven.
- **KNOWN LIMITATION** — a property of the current design that operators must plan around.

**Count:** 37 open gaps — 9 EXTERNAL PREREQUISITE, 10 NOT BUILT, 4 NOT VERIFIED, 14 KNOWN
LIMITATION. 2 closed.
